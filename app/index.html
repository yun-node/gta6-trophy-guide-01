      Roadmap to the Platinum — about 200 hours

      One playthrough is enough, there is no mission replay, and there are no online or difficulty trophies. PowerPyx rates the set 4/10 and about 200 hours: 60–80 for the story, about 100 for the 100% checklist, about 10 for the last cleanup. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off. The game gives you 15 manual save slots — keep one at the start of every chapter.

        Missable trophies

        There is no mission replay and no New Game+, so the only protection is a manual save at the start of each chapter. The list below is built from the official descriptions and the published guides — and it is short on purpose.

        Not missable — don’t worry about these

        • All 12 story trophies unlock automatically as you finish chapters and missions.
        • Every side-character questline (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret) stays available after the story, even if its giver goes quiet for a chapter or two.
        • Optional missions that disappear do not affect any trophy; the 100% checklist needs only 100 of the 200+ optional missions.
        • Some fences and magazine collectibles in story buildings disappear, but neither affects a trophy — new fences unlock and the magazines spawn elsewhere.

        Sources: PowerPyx — GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap · Output Lag — GTA 6 Trophy List

        About this guide

        Grand Theft Auto VI, Rockstar’s return to Vice City and the state of Leonida, stars Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — an easy score gone wrong, a conspiracy across the state, and the two of them relying on each other to get out alive. That is Rockstar’s own synopsis, from its official GTA VI page.

        The trophy list is the official PlayStation 5 list as published on the trackers Exophase and PSNProfiles: 55 trophies — 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — 28 of them hidden until earned, all of them single-player. Names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as published. The guides are written in this guide’s own words, each citing the source its facts come from; where no published guide has confirmed a method yet, the guide says so instead of guessing.

        How your progress is stored

        Ticking a trophy saves it in your browser (localStorage) on this device only. Export downloads a JSON file of your ticks; Import reads one back. Nothing leaves your machine — the app makes no network requests at all.
